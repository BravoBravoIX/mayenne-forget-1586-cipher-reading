# v0.2.1

## Abstract

First reading of the whole cipher letter, partial in places. Key: S. Tomokiyo's reconstruction, with code values aligned on five letters deciphered at the time in the same volume.

## Results in brief

- Both pages of BnF fr. 15573 f. 385 are read: 68 lines, 2052 signs, five reader passes combined by vote, then corrections checked on the image.
- On f. 385r, 0.921 of the signs read as French; on f. 385v, 0.929. Shuffled controls score 0.59–0.62, and the known-answer lines score 0.868.
- Five other letters in this cipher, deciphered at the time, give values for six code signs (*armée*, *tout*, *ée*, *tant*, and *tre* and *droit*, both doubtful).
- 89 items remain open: 12 code signs without a value, 41 doubtful words and 36 short stretches not yet read.
- The evidence weighs toward a letter from the royal army in Guyenne breaking up at the start of the winter of 1586.
